# Sermon Prep Assistant — demo script

**Target runtime: 3:50.** Open the app in a fresh browser tab at 100% zoom. Have a blank local text editor ready for the copy demonstration. Close unrelated tabs and notifications before recording.

| Time | On-screen actions | Spoken lines |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0:00–0:25 · Intro** | Show the landing page and the three prep steps. | “This is Sermon Prep Assistant, a private study workspace for pastors in the Discipleship and Spiritual Growth track. It helps you move from a passage or a theme to an editable starting point for a sermon.” |
| **0:25–0:50 · Problem** | Point out the topic box and example buttons. Do not generate yet. | “A busy week can leave a pastor staring at a blank page. Starting with a workable outline can help, but tools that invent a verse or send private notes to an outside service introduce new problems. Here, the draft starts from Scripture you can check.” |
| **0:50–1:35 · Generate** | Type `John 3:16` into the passage field and click **Generate**. Scroll through the three numbered points and down to the closing prayer. | “I’ll start with John three sixteen. The draft gives me a title, Scripture focus, introduction, and exactly three main points. Each point has a King James citation, an explanation, an illustration, and questions I can adapt for my congregation. A call to response and a short closing prayer round it out.” |
| **1:35–2:15 · Edit and export** | Return to the draft title and replace it with `Loved Before We Earned It`. Click **Copy Outline**, then paste into the local text editor to show the revised title and disclosure. Return to the app, click **Print / Save as PDF**, show the draft-only preview, and cancel. | “I’m changing the title right here. The other fields are editable too, including the questions and prayer. Copy includes the changes I’ve made and keeps the draft label. Print lets me bring the outline to my desk or save it as a PDF.” |
| **2:15–2:55 · Verse lookup** | Under **Verse lookup**, search `John 3:16`. Replace it with `hope` and search again. Show a result with its KJV citation. | “Verse lookup is separate, so I don’t lose my draft while studying. I can look up John three sixteen by reference or search for a theme like hope. These are the words in the built-in King James library, not generated Bible quotations.” |
| **2:55–3:30 · Honesty and privacy** | Point to the disclosure in the draft and the note in the sidebar. | “The page and exported outline plainly say: AI-assisted draft — review, pray over, and edit before preaching. The pastor is responsible for the message and should check every verse in their own Bible. The tool uses local rules, not a remote AI model. It sends no sermon content to a server and has no accounts or analytics.” |
| **3:30–3:50 · Closing** | Click the **Psalm 23** example to show another one-click draft. Leave its title visible. | “Psalm twenty-three is one click away. The goal is not to write a pastor’s sermon for them. It’s to make the first step less daunting and leave more time to read, pray, and prepare for the people they serve.” |
